    The 19 largest cities

    NCRB publishes these figures for the 19 cities that had two million people or more in the 2011 census. Its rate divides each city's cases by the city's 2011 census population, which has not been revised since, so for a city that has grown the rate is higher than it would be on a current population. The rate is shown as NCRB prints it and is not comparable with the state rates on this site. NCRB Crime in India, table 3B.1.
