Protests

The protests of September 2026

In the third week of September 2026, cases of sexual violence against girls in Bihar and Delhi, reported days apart, brought students and women's groups onto the streets. This page sets out what was reported and names the outlet that reported it. It leaves out any detail that could identify the girls.

What was reported

  1. 19 September · Jamui, Bihar

    A girl and a boy, both minors, were stopped by a group of men on a road in Jamui district. The men groped and harassed the girl and beat the boy. A video of the assault circulated two days later, and the police registered a case under the POCSO Act on their own initiative. The Hindu reported arrests in the case.

    The Hindu · The Indian Express · BBC

  2. September · Samastipur, Bihar

    Days later a second video, from Samastipur district, showed a group of young men surrounding and harassing a girl who appeared to be a minor. The Times of India reported that the police arrested four men.

    The Times of India

  3. September · Delhi

    In the same week a girl was allegedly gang-raped by three men in a park in Delhi. The News Mill, reporting with ANI, said that arrests were made.

    The News Mill and ANI

  4. September · Delhi University

    Students of Lady Shri Ram College, Gargi College, Miranda House and Kamala Nehru College marched near August Kranti Marg and then met on Siri Fort Road to draft a charter of demands, The Indian Express reported.

    The Indian Express

  5. What the students asked for

    Lady Shri Ram College briefly moved its classes online. The organisers criticised a reported move by the Delhi Development Authority to bar women from public parks after sunset, and stricter hostel and PG curfews. They asked for working street lights, more patrolling, accessible bus and metro passes, the education of men and boys, and the punishment of offenders.

    The Indian Express · The News Mill and ANI · The Guardian
