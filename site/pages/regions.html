    NCRB publishes no figures for regions. The five Zonal Councils were created under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, and the North Eastern Council is a separate body. This page groups the states by the membership of those councils as the Press Information Bureau listed it on 31 January 2017. Ladakh was not a union territory then. It was part of Jammu and Kashmir until 2019 and is placed with Northern, as Jammu and Kashmir is. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu was formed in 2020 from two union territories that the list places in Western, and it is placed there. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep are not on the list and are shown apart. A region's total adds its state rows, and its rate divides the summed cases by the summed number of women.
