The eight North-Eastern states side by side, from the cases police recorded and NCRB published. They are listed in alphabetical order and are not ranked, because a high recorded rate can come from more crime or from more recording, and the figures cannot tell the two apart.

Cases for every lakh women, year by year

NCRB publishes no figures for regions. The North-East total here adds the rows of the eight states of the North Eastern Council, and its rate divides the summed cases by the summed number of women. NCRB's state tables give no figure below the state, so there is no district view.
