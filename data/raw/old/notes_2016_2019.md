# NCRB 2016-2019: extraction notes (spot-checked)

## Spot checks
All India rape: 32,559 (2017), 33,356 (2018), 32,033 (2019). Total for 2019: 4,05,861. These match NCRB's published figures.

## Checks run
- 194 checks passed and 1 failed.
- Every count column adds to NCRB's printed totals.
- Every rate equals cases over female population.
- The 3A.1 total equals the 3A.2 total and the 3A.6 cases reported.
- Each IPC total equals the sum of its heads, and IPC plus SLL equals the total.
- The one failure is NCRB's own: Uttarakhand trafficking in 2016 prints a rate of 0.1 where 2 cases over 52.2 lakh women gives 0.04.

## Layouts
- 2017-2019 use the same column numbers as 2020-2023.
- 2016 uses a different layout.

## 2016 is not comparable on its main heads
- Rape includes POCSO s.4 and 6 (38,947 cases, against 32,559 in 2017).
- There is no POCSO in the crime against women tables, so the 2016 SLL total and the total exclude it.
- There is no murder with rape head.
- Acid attack and attempted acid attack are one head.
- Cyber covers s.67A only.
- Abetment of suicide covers s.306 only.
- Courts print acquitted and discharged as one figure.

## Every year from 2016 to 2019
- Jammu & Kashmir includes Ladakh, and no Ladakh row is printed.
- D&N Haveli and Daman & Diu are printed separately.

## Population behind the rates
- 2016-2017: "Registrar General of India estimated population of 2016 [2017] based on 2001 Census" (quoted verbatim).
- 2018-2019: the Technical Group on Population Projections (November 2019).
- The 3.6% jump in the All India female population between 2017 and 2018 is that change of source.

## Year notes
- 2018 every page: "# Clarifications are pending from West Bengal, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya & Sikkim".
- 2019 every page: "Due to non-receipt of data from West Bengal in time for 2019, Data furnished for 2018 has been used". West Bengal's 2019 case and victim counts are identical to its 2018 counts.
- Revisions: the 2019 edition revised Assam 2018 from 27,728 to 27,687, and All India 2018 from 378,277 to 378,236.
