# Code of Conduct

Women on Record handles records of violence against women, and the people who
read it and work on it include survivors. We expect everyone to be respectful and
careful, to describe cases only by their legal title, never to name, picture or
describe a survivor, and to accept correction. Harassment, personal attacks and
discriminatory language are not acceptable.

Report concerns to care@pinpointventures.in. We will acknowledge a report within
48 hours.
