Crimes against women in India, as police and courts recorded them

This site covers every state and union territory from 2020 to 2024, using the National Crime Records Bureau's Crime in India reports. It counts the cases that reached a police station and were written down there, and many crimes against women never reach a police station.

The protests of September 2026

In the third week of September 2026, cases of sexual violence against girls in Bihar and Delhi, reported days apart, brought students and women's groups onto the streets. This section sets out what was reported and names the outlet that reported it. It leaves out any detail that could identify the girls.

  1. 19 September · Jamui, Bihar

    A schoolgirl and a schoolboy, both minors, were stopped by a group of men on a road in Jamui district in the evening. The men groped and harassed the girl and beat the boy. A video of the assault circulated two days later, and the police registered a case under the POCSO Act on their own initiative. The Hindu reported that all seven accused had been arrested.

    The Hindu · The Indian Express · BBC

  2. September · Samastipur, Bihar

    Days later a second video, from Samastipur district, showed a group of young men surrounding and harassing a girl who appeared to be a minor. The Times of India reported that the police arrested four men.

    The Times of India

  3. September · South Delhi

    In the same week a girl of 17 was allegedly gang-raped by three men in a park in south Delhi, near Lady Shri Ram College. The News Mill, reporting with ANI, said that arrests were made.

    The News Mill and ANI

  4. 25 September · Delhi University

    Students of Lady Shri Ram College, Gargi College, Miranda House and Kamala Nehru College marched for the second time in a week. About 200 students marched near August Kranti Marg and then met on Siri Fort Road to draft a charter of demands, The Indian Express reported.

    The Indian Express

  5. What the students asked for

    Lady Shri Ram College briefly moved its classes online. The organisers criticised a reported move by the Delhi Development Authority to bar women from public parks after sunset, and stricter hostel and PG curfews. They asked for working street lights, more patrolling, accessible bus and metro passes, the education of men and boys, and the punishment of offenders.

    The Indian Express · The News Mill and ANI · The Guardian

    Each crime, five years, India

    Each chart shows the cases recorded in India in each year from 2020 to 2024. Counts fell everywhere in 2020, when the lockdown kept people away from police stations. The new criminal laws came into force in the middle of 2024, and the 2024 counts add the cases booked under the old laws and the new ones.

    Read this before quoting a number

    1. These figures count the cases that police recorded, which is a smaller number than the crimes that took place. The National Family Health Survey asks women directly, and in its 2015-16 round 77 per cent of women who had experienced physical or sexual violence had never sought help or told anyone about it (NFHS-4 India report, p. 572).
    2. NCRB counts each FIR once, under the most serious offence in it. A rape that ended in a murder is therefore counted once, as murder with rape.
    3. Rape of girls under 18 is mostly booked under the POCSO Act. The rape figures and the POCSO figures are separate series and should be read together.
    4. From 1 July 2024 offences were booked under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. NCRB reports 2024 under both codes, and this site adds the two together. Where NCRB split one older heading into several, the parts are added back so that the five years can be compared, and each such sum is named in the dataset.
    5. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu merged in January 2020. Ladakh became a union territory in 2019. All five years use today's 36 states and union territories.

    The data, free to use

    The files below hold every number on this page, with the NCRB table it came from, the section of law it falls under, and a note wherever a figure needs one. You may use them freely if you cite NCRB and link to this site.

    Sources and method

    Where every number comes from

    National Crime Records Bureau, Crime in India, Volume 1, for 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024: tables 2A.3 (murder victims by sex), 3A.1 to 3A.3 (crimes against women and rape victims by age), 3A.5 to 3A.8 (police and court disposal).

      How it is checked

      A script read every table directly from NCRB's PDFs. For every count, the 28 states must add up to NCRB's own total for states, the 8 union territories must add up to its total for union territories, and the two together must equal the all-India total. They do in every table and every year. The yearly all-India totals in table 3A.2 were also matched against the totals NCRB prints in table 3A.1.

      No one is named

      This site holds counts only. It does not name, picture or describe any survivor, including in the cases that started the protests. Section 72 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, earlier section 228A of the IPC, makes disclosing a rape survivor's identity an offence.
